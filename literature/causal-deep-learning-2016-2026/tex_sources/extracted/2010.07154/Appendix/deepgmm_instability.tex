In the dSprite experiments, we observed that the training procedure of DeepGMM can be unstable.  Figure~\ref{fig:dSprite-deepgmm} displays the MSE for the models learned in the first 100 epochs. Here, we can see that DeepGMM performs poorly in the early stage of the learning. Furthermore, even after it appears to have converged, we observe a sudden increase of MSE around 80th epoch, which makes difficult to determine when to stop. We conjecture that this is due to the instability of the smooth zero-sum game solved by DeepGMM. By contrast, DFIV converges quickly and performs consistently better than DeepGMM on this task.
