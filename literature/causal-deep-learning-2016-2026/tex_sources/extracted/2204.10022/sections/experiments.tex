\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}
Here we empirically validate our method.
First, we consider a synthetic structural causal model (SCM) to demonstrate the validity of our method.
Next, we show the scalability of our methods by applying them to a real-world climate-science-inspired problem.
Implementation details (\cref{app:implementation}), datasets (\cref{app:datasets}), and code are provided at 
\url{https://github.com/oatml/overcast}.
